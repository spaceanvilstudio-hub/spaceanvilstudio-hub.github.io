Support

Need help with CalcForge? Please use the support contact published with the official CalcForge app listing and include the calculator name, the steps that produced the issue, and your device/Android version when relevant.

Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in a support request.

Useful information to include

  • The calculator you were using.
  • The inputs or general steps that led to the issue.
  • The expected result and what CalcForge displayed instead.
  • Your CalcForge app version and Android version, when known.

Privacy requests

For a privacy question or request concerning information under CalcForge's control, use the same official support route and clearly identify it as a privacy request.
