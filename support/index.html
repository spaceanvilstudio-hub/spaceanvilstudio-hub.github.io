Support

Need help with CalcForge? Please use the support contact published with the official CalcForge app listing and include the calculator name, the steps that produced the issue, and your device/Android version when relevant.

Please do not send passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information in a support request.

Useful information to include

  • The calculator you were using.
  • The inputs or general steps that led to the issue.
  • The expected result and what CalcForge displayed instead.
  • Your CalcForge app version and Android version, when known.

Privacy and data deletion requests

For a privacy question or request concerning information under CalcForge's control, contact spaceanvil.studio@gmail.com and clearly identify the request as a privacy or data deletion request.

To request deletion, send an email with the subject CalcForge data deletion request and include enough information for us to identify the relevant support correspondence or other information under CalcForge's control. Do not include passwords, payment credentials, government identification numbers, or other sensitive information.

Data that can be deleted includes information voluntarily provided in a support request. Locally stored calculator history, favorites, and preferences remain on the user's device and are deleted by the user through the app's available controls, by clearing app data, or by uninstalling CalcForge. Advertising data handled by Google Mobile Ads is governed by Google's own data retention and deletion practices.

We retain support correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary to respond to requests, resolve issues, maintain appropriate records, or comply with legal obligations.
