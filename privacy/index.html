Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 October 2026

CalcForge is designed so that calculator inputs and results can be processed locally on your device. Advertising and certain platform services may process limited device and advertising information as described below.

1. Information handled by CalcForge

Calculator inputs and results are primarily processed on your device. When you use features such as calculation history or favorites, the app may store the information you choose to keep locally on your device.

When you share a calculation using your device's sharing features, the information you choose to share is passed to the operating system or the destination you select.

2. Advertising

The Android release of CalcForge uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertising. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, app and device information, and advertising-related signals as permitted by applicable settings and consent choices.

CalcForge uses consent-management controls where required by applicable privacy rules. Your choices may affect whether personalized or non-personalized advertising is shown.

3. Diagnostics and support

CalcForge may record limited diagnostic information needed to investigate errors or support requests when such features are enabled. CalcForge does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information through calculator inputs.

Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support may be used to respond to your request and maintain the relevant support correspondence.

4. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored calculator history and favorites remain on your device until you remove them, clear app data, or uninstall CalcForge. Information handled by third-party advertising or platform services is subject to those providers' own retention policies.

5. Third-party services

CalcForge may use third-party platform services needed for advertising, app distribution, or device functionality. Those services process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable law.

6. Children

CalcForge is not directed specifically to children. The app should be used in accordance with the age requirements and policies of the platform from which it is installed.

7. Privacy choices and requests

Where a consent dialog or privacy setting is provided, you can use it to review or change available advertising consent choices. You may also request information about privacy handling or deletion of information under CalcForge's control through the official support route.

8. International processing

Third-party services may process information in countries other than the country where you live, subject to their privacy terms and applicable safeguards.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when CalcForge's data practices, services, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top indicates the current version.
